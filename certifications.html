Professional development

CGIPD · CGIIC · CGIMM · CMGI

Four professional credentials that start with the stormwater BMPs engineers, contractors, facility managers and inspectors work with every day — basins, bioretention, swales, permeable pavement — and go further, into connected green infrastructure that delivers cleaner water, flood resilience, cooler streets and lasting value. Earn and collect Planning & Design, Installation & Construction, Maintenance & Management, and the Master of Green Infrastructure. Each is more than 30 hours toward the PDU, CEU and PDH requirements of your other credentials.

Continuing education built in

Earn a new credential — and keep your others current

Each MAWA credential is more than 30 hours of documented, self-paced professional development (more than 120 hours for all four). Use your dated certificate toward the continuing-education requirements of the credentials you already hold:

PDUs — CPESC, CPSWQ, CESSWI and other stormwater & inspector certifications
PDHs — Professional Engineer license renewal
CEUs / CE hours — LEED, ENV SP, landscape architecture, arborist and floodplain credentials
CM credits — AICP certification maintenance

Each credentialing body decides which courses and how many hours it accepts — confirm with yours before submitting.
