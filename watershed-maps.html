How to use the maps

Explore

Drag to turn the county, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click a watershed to see the subwatersheds inside it. Orange dots show which way rain runs off the land.

Download and print

Save a high-resolution image or PDF, or print straight from the map. Every copy carries the Mid-America Watershed.Org logo and data credits.

Share a view

The Share button copies a link that opens the exact county, watershed and subwatershed you are looking at.

For planners and schools

Use the maps in watershed plans, stormwater presentations, board packets and lessons on how water moves across the land.

Need a county that isn't here yet?

Planning agencies, conservation districts and schools can request a county and we'll move it up the list.
