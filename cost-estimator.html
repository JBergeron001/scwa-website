Sources, assumptions and every number used

Planning-level screening estimates, not bids. Range is the lowest to highest published figure; typical is the middle (median) figure. Each figure is converted to 2025 dollars with the U.S. Consumer Price Index (annual averages). St. Louis is more than 100 miles from the regional cost centers the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency uses, so the national cost level (1.0) applies. Design, engineering and contingency are added on top of build figures because several sources exclude them. Life-cycle cost adds the present value of yearly upkeep over the design life; replacement costs are not included because the sources give no replacement intervals. Gallons managed assume about 42 inches of rain a year, 90% runoff from impervious surfaces, and practices sized to capture about 85% of yearly runoff (green roofs retain about half the rain that falls on them; cisterns are assumed to reuse 30%). Default practice sizes are common planning rules of thumb; replace them with your design.
