Framework & vocabulary

A shared language for BMP performance over time

Three working concepts MAWA uses across its tools and standards to name both the problem — and the fix — when green infrastructure drifts from its design intent.

The problem to prevent

Sustainable Functionality Degradation (SFD)

Green infrastructure that looks and works well at install but loses function over time, as materials, design, maintenance and site conditions fall out of alignment. Naming SFD makes slow failure visible — and fundable — before a practice has to be declassified.

The approach

Adaptive Materials Mapping (AMM)

Choosing materials and construction style to fit the specific site rather than a generic template — matching each practice to the environmental context it actually has to live in.

The approach

Contextual Material Optimization (CMO)

Selecting and tuning materials for a project's unique environmental and site-specific factors — optimizing long-term performance and sustainability, not just day-one appearance.

These aren't just labels. The MW-1 Technical Standard names the context-matched design targets (AMM / CMO) and the failure modes (SFD) for each practice family — so degradation is addressed in design, permits and plan review rather than discovered at failure.
